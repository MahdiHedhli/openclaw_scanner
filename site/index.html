Authorized Use Only

This is not a public vulnerability scanner. The checker requires an authorization acknowledgement before any scan request.

Evidence First

Discovery, family match, exact version evidence, and vulnerability context stay separate. Passive guesses are not promoted.

Low Impact

The backend is restricted to safe remote-visible checks. It does not authenticate, execute payloads, attach debuggers, or interact with VNC.

Do not scan IPs, hostnames, or services you are not authorized to assess. Abuse may be rate-limited or blocked.
